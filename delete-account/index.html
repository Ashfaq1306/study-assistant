Delete your Study Mate account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Study Mate and sign in.
  2. Tap your profile picture at the top right.
  3. Choose Delete account.
  4. Enter your password, tick the box and tap Delete my account.

If your apps are locked right now, pass your exam (or ask the parent who set the lock to lift it) first: an account can't be deleted while a lock is running.

Without the app

Email us from the email address (or tell us the mobile number) on your account, at the address shown on Study Mate's Google Play page, with the subject "Delete my account". We will confirm it is your account and delete it within 7 days.

What is deleted

Everything, right away and for good:

  • Your profile, email address and mobile number
  • Study sessions, study time, notes and photos
  • Exams, answers, results and AI helper chats
  • Your calendar and reminders
  • Your phones, screen-time records, family links, and locks set by or for you

Nothing is kept after deletion, except short-lived server security logs (IP addresses and times), which are removed automatically within 30 days. See our privacy policy.
