Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Study Assistant helps students focus: they choose what to study, lock distracting apps, and unlock them by passing a short quiz. Parents can link to their child's account. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We never sell your data and we show no ads.

What we collect

  • Account: name, email address and/or mobile number, date of birth (to apply the age rules below), role (student or parent), and a hashed password. We never store your password itself.
  • Study data: subjects, topics and notes you enter, study times, exam settings, questions, your answers and results, and photos of study pages you choose to take.
  • AI helper chats: the questions you ask the study helper and its answers.
  • Calendar: exams, classes and reminders you add or import (including any calendar file or photo you upload for import).
  • App lock (students, only if switched on): the list of apps installed on the phone (names and package names), which app is on screen (only its package name, to know when a locked app opens), and, with your permission, how long apps were used each day (screen time). The app never reads what you type, your messages or anything inside other apps.
  • Device and technical data: phone model, app version, whether app lock and usage access are on, the time your phone last checked in, and IP addresses and sign-in times (to keep accounts secure and show sign-in history).

How we use it

  • To run the app: accounts, study sessions, locks, exams, the AI helper, the calendar and reminders.
  • To let a linked parent see their child's study time, screen time, exam results and lock status, and set locks and questions.
  • To keep the service safe: sign-in protection, preventing abuse, and support.
  • To send one-time sign-in codes by email or SMS.

AI features

Exam questions, marking of short answers, the study helper and calendar import are produced by Google's Gemini AI. To do this, the relevant text (and study photos or calendar files you add) is sent to Google, which processes it for us. AI can make mistakes: every AI answer and exam question has a Report button, and reports are reviewed by us.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers needed to run the app, under their terms:

  • Google (Gemini API) for AI features, and Google (Gmail) to email sign-in codes.
  • Cloudflare, which carries the encrypted connection between the app and our server.
  • An SMS provider, if you choose to receive codes by text message.

We share data with a linked parent as described above, and otherwise only if the law requires it.

Children

Study Assistant is made for students and their families. Students under 15 must have a parent link to their account before they can use the study features, and the parent can see their study and lock information. Parent accounts are for adults (18+). If you are a parent and want your child's data deleted, delete the child's account in the app or contact us.

Security

All connections use HTTPS. Passwords are stored as scrypt hashes, one-time codes only as keyed hashes, and sign-in tokens expire and rotate. Access to our administration tools is limited to the app's operator.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, we delete it and everything in it right away (profile, study history, photos, exams, chats, calendar, devices and family links). Server logs are kept for a short time for security and then removed.

Your choices

  • Delete your account: in the app, open the account menu (top right) and choose Delete account. See how to delete your account.
  • Switch off app lock, usage access or notifications at any time in your phone's settings (app lock can't be switched off while a lock is running).
  • Ask us for a copy of your data or to correct it.

Contact

Questions or requests: email us at the address shown on Study Assistant's Google Play page.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
